## Overview

TAN is a B2B sales and relationship selling course. It teaches professionals — consultants,
advisors, account managers, coaches — how to build deep trust-based client relationships that
lead to long-term engagements, referrals, and premium positioning.

The course lives on the CoachingOS.com domain.
